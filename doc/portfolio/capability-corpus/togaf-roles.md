# TOGAF roles

The ai4X capability corpus draws on The Open Group’s *Architecture Roles and Skills*. We used the [role descriptions in chapter 4](https://pubs.opengroup.org/togaf-standard/architecture-roles-and-skills/chap04.html) to identify who is accountable for which architecture decisions, and the [skill matrix in chapter 5](https://pubs.opengroup.org/togaf-standard/architecture-roles-and-skills/chap05.html#tbl-Skill-Architecture-Role_Matrix) to identify the skills each role needs. The resulting capabilities are maintained in the [architecture family](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/).

| Role | Responsibility in the ai4X curation | Capabilities in the corpus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chief and Enterprise Architect | The Enterprise Architect integrates architecture across segments, ensures interoperability and aligns principles, governance and roadmaps. The Chief Architect takes that enterprise-wide remit to executive and board level and is accountable for the architecture landscape and its governance. We use the Enterprise skills column for both. | [architecture-practice-leadership](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/enterprise/architecture-practice-leadership.md), [enterprise-architecture-integration](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/enterprise/enterprise-architecture-integration.md), [togaf-adm-discipline](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/enterprise/togaf-adm-discipline.md), [capability-map-governance](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/enterprise/capability-map-governance.md), [architecture-compliance-review](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/governance/architecture-compliance-review.md) |
| Segment Architect | Stewards one or more business or technical segments, sets their policies, standards, roadmaps and working practices, and coordinates specialists and Solution Architects. | [segment-architecture-stewardship](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/domain/segment-architecture-stewardship.md) |
| Business domain | Models business capabilities and develops business services. | [business-service-architecture](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/domain/business-service-architecture.md) |
| Data domain | Governs data ownership, integration, migration, protection and master data. | [data-governance-architecture](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/domain/data-governance-architecture.md) |
| Application domain | Decomposes applications into services and defines how they integrate and interoperate. | [application-integration-architecture](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/domain/application-integration-architecture.md) |
| Technology domain | Integrates platforms, networks, devices and data-center infrastructure. | [technology-platform-architecture](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/domain/technology-platform-architecture.md) |
| Solution Architect | Guides delivery of new or changed capabilities by defining solution topology and interfaces, planning the transition from baseline to target, and governing implementation risks. | [solution-realization-architecture](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/solution/solution-realization-architecture.md), [baseline-target-gap-analysis](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/solution/baseline-target-gap-analysis.md), [transition-architecture-planning](../../../src/resources/corpus/capability/architecture/solution/transition-architecture-planning.md) |

In this curation, Business, Data, Application and Technology are four specializations of the Segment Architect. They combine the segment responsibility from chapter 4 with the corresponding domain skills from chapter 5.

## AI-specific additions

Architects can select these five ai4X additions when their assignment calls for them. The operating-model contract assigns organizational responsibilities. The other four guide solution design and architecture governance.
