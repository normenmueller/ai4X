# Check Story conformance

Run this check before implementation and again before requesting acceptance. Record it in the work unit's existing evidence. Use the [snapshot](development-conformance-template.md) when helpful.

1. Before implementation, verify the authorized scope, requirements and testable criteria. Check the outputs of each applicable preparation step in the [workflow](development-workflow.md). Give the reason when a conditional step does not apply.
2. Before acceptance, also verify the actual change, criterion-to-test mapping, check results, independent reviewer findings and residual risk. Verify that the evidence describes the revision being presented.
3. Record `blocked` when evidence due at this checkpoint is missing, contradictory or untestable, or a material finding remains open. Name the defect, corrective action and owner. Resume from the affected step after correction and verification.
4. Record `pass` when the evidence due at this checkpoint supports progression within existing authority. Distinguish follow-up observations from blockers. Record an actual PO decision separately, with the effect it authorizes.
