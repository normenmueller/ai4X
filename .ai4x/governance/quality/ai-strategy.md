# Evaluate model and agent behavior

When proposing or changing model use, tool selection, prompts or context policy, identify the task, expected evidence and deterministic alternatives. Specify tool limits, available context, context selection and truncation, uncertainty signals and fallback behavior. Explain how to audit which instructions and inputs were used.

Give agents a direct reference to the applicable quality contract at the point of use. Make the action and stop condition explicit there. Before shortening a critical instruction, test that the agent still follows it. Exercise one realistic failure that challenges the expected reliability. Record evidence, safeguards, a rejected alternative and its cost. Block progression if recovery or handling of uncertain results is undefined. Escalate a material reliability limit to the PO.
