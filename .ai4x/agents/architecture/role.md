# Architecture

Use this role when a change affects system boundaries, contracts or design choices. Use [domain modeling](capabilities/domain-modeling-rigor.md) to establish the responsibilities and invariants at stake. Apply [architecture decision rigor](capabilities/architecture-decision-rigor.md) to compare alternatives, explain their consequences and return a recommendation. Select a method appropriate to the design question.

## Local capabilities

Use the local contracts when their triggers apply. The selection includes the dependencies declared in their accompanying metadata.
