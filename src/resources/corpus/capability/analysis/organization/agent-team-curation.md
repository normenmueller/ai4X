# Agent and Team Curation (MUST)

## Purpose

Compose an agent or a team whose capabilities and working arrangements fit a confirmed need.

## Trigger

Use when the requester has confirmed why a change matters and what outcome is needed, and a proposal for an agent or team is wanted.

## Rules (MUST)

- Derive the work the outcome requires and the evidence of success. Search available capability contracts and reusable agent profiles; record the candidate IDs or names, revisions and relevant evidence. An existing profile can cover several jobs. Check its actual composition against the need.
- For each identified candidate explain the selection or exclusion, the job it covers, its boundaries and its dependencies or conflicts. Identify uncovered work explicitly. Separate cognitive abilities from optional technical support.
- Compare the smallest viable single-agent composition with a team when an independent review, distinct authority or other specific need could justify a handoff. State why the chosen arrangement fits. Build a new composition when existing profiles only partly fit.
- For a team, specify who owns each job, who collaborates or reviews, and which decisions need human authority. Describe how work starts, what each handoff carries, what acceptance requires and who handles an absent role or a blocked decision. For a single agent, state its mandate and how it reaches the authorized person at its boundary.
- Present the proposal with its rationale, gaps, uncertainty and decision points. Distinguish a proposal from a verified composition and from human acceptance. Request a decision on the exact reviewable proposal.

## Fallback

If purpose or outcome changes, return to the requester for confirmation. When an essential job lacks a suitable capability, report the uncovered job, closest candidates and what they lack. Keep the affected proposal blocked until the gap or scope is resolved.

## Minimal Output Contract

A traceable proposal for one agent or a team, the rationale for each candidate disposition, operating arrangements, unresolved gaps and the human decision required, or a specific blocking gap.
