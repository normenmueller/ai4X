# Resolve conflicting rules

Draft for review.

When two rules appear to require different actions, check that both apply to this task, revision and decision. Apply both if their requirements can be satisfied together.

If satisfying one would violate the other, follow the precedence explicitly established by the authority governing that decision. Identify the provision establishing precedence. A more specific rule can add a requirement; specificity alone grants no exception.

If precedence is unresolved, pause the affected action. Give the designated decision owner both clauses, the case in which they require incompatible actions, and a recommendation with its reason. Resume that action once an authorized decision is recorded at its owner.
